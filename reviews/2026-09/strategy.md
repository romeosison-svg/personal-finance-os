## Travel And Plan It Strategy

The Turkey trip created two different planning patterns:

1. Known large annual-trip costs, such as BA Holidays, may be suitable for Plan It when the fee is explicit and the term matches the useful life of the trip.
2. Cash needs, such as EUR exchange and Monzo TRY float, must still be funded from current cash before travel.

Future rule candidate:

- Larger annual-trip purchases can use 12-month Plan It where the fee is acceptable and the monthly payment is included in the plan.
- Smaller travel costs should prefer 3 months or cash, unless 12 months is needed for a clearly documented cashflow reason.
- Plan It should not make unfunded travel feel affordable; the monthly fee and all existing card obligations still count in affordability.
