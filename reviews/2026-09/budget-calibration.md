## Calibration Principles

1. Credit cards remain payment rails, not spending buffers.
2. Every card transaction must map to a funded cash bucket.
3. One-off or unusually high items are recorded but do not automatically become recurring baseline limits.
4. Santander recurring bills and vehicle commitments are fixed obligations, not controllable lifestyle buckets.
5. Turkey trip cash is a known one-off September bucket because it must be funded before travel.
6. Balance-transfer direct debits and card repayments are outside this phase.
7. Bucket limits are set before affordability is tested.

| Dining / cafe / fast food on Amex | Reduces dining / takeaway / coffee bucket |
| Sport / fitness on Amex, Halifax, or current account | Reduces the relevant personal sport bucket unless explicitly shared |

| Event tickets on card | Reduces entertainment bucket unless documented as a one-off |
