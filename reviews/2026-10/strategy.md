## Fully Funded 0% Purchase Reserve

A future major purchase may use 0% finance without undermining the no-new-debt objective only when it is fully liability-matched.

Proposed durable rule:

1. The purchase must be independently approved as affordable at its full cash price.
2. Ring-fence 100% of the net purchase price in a dedicated accessible cash reserve on day one.
3. Confirm that total repayments equal the best available cash price and that there are no setup, mandatory-upgrade, or settlement costs that negate the benefit.
4. Keep the reserve in cash, not in the GIA or another market-risk investment.
5. Pay the finance DD from Lloyds and transfer the exact monthly amount from the reserve before collection.
6. Reconcile the reserve to the outstanding finance balance monthly.
7. Do not use the reserve for another purpose until the liability is cleared.
8. Consider the credit-file effect before applying, particularly ahead of any mortgage application or refinancing.

## Pension Strategy

Do not opt out of Romeo's workplace pension solely to increase monthly lifestyle cash. Workplace pension membership ordinarily includes employer contributions and tax relief; opting out can forfeit employer contributions. UK guidance also notes that reducing contributions temporarily may be possible depending on the scheme.

Before changing the pension:

1. Obtain the scheme contribution schedule and confirm Romeo's employee and employer percentages.
2. Confirm whether contributions use salary sacrifice, net pay, or relief at source.
3. Calculate the actual increase in take-home pay from reducing or stopping contributions.
4. Compare that cash increase with the employer contribution and tax/NI benefits lost.
5. Consider a temporary reduction only if future monthly reviews show a structural cashflow deficit after the debt reset and cash-bucket trial.

Current strategic recommendation: remain enrolled. The October position no longer supports the earlier argument that opting out is necessary to prevent immediate consumer-debt escalation.

Reference: [GOV.UK workplace pension guidance](https://www.gov.uk/workplace-pensions) and [GOV.UK leaving a workplace pension](https://www.gov.uk/workplace-pensions/if-you-want-to-leave-your-workplace-pension-scheme).
