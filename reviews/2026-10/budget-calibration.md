## Calibration Principles

1. Credit cards are payment rails; every new purchase consumes a funded category bucket.
2. Statement-period travel connected to completed or past bookings does not become a recurring monthly baseline.
3. Interest and debt repayments are not lifestyle buckets.
4. Mortgage, Santander recurring outflows, fixed current-account DDs, tithe, and card/BT obligations remain outside controllable-spend buckets.
5. Visible card groceries do not represent the entire household because Santander activity is missing; grocery headroom is retained for that limitation.
6. Personal sport, health, shopping, and discretionary limits remain owner-specific. Shared food, transport, entertainment, and household spending remain household buckets.
7. Limits are calibrated before affordability is tested.
