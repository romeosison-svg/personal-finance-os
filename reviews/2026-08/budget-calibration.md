## 2. Calibration Principles

1. Credit cards remain payment rails, not spending buffers.
2. Every card transaction must map to a funded cash bucket.
3. The same amount should be mirrored into a card-payment holding bucket when a credit card is used.
4. One-off or unusually high items are recorded but do not automatically become the recurring baseline.
5. Bucket limits are set before affordability is tested.
6. Balance-transfer direct debits and card repayments are outside this phase.

## 11. Card-Use Tracking Rule

If Amex or another credit card is used for points or convenience, the transaction still consumes the relevant cash bucket.

The operating rule for later phases is:

| Card use | Bucket treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Grocery spend on Amex | Reduces groceries bucket |
| Dining / cafe / fast food on Amex | Reduces the matching food-out bucket |
| Sport / fitness on Amex or Barclaycard | Reduces personal sport bucket |
| Work or business services on card | Reduces Romeo business / professional bucket |
| Event tickets on card | Reduces entertainment bucket unless flagged as one-off |

This is a tracking rule only. Exact account movements belong to Plan.
