## Operating Buffer Strategy

The November clarification does not support replacing every £500 buffer with £250 automatically. It supports a repeatable two-stage test:

1. Start each review with the normal £500 buffer.
2. Use £1,000 instead if an elevated-risk trigger applies.
3. After obligations, current liabilities and calibrated buckets are tested, reduce the operative buffer to £250 only when:
   - household income is stable;
   - the emergency fund is at or above target;
   - all mandatory obligations and current card liabilities are funded;
   - no elevated-risk trigger applies; and
   - the Plan remains cash-positive at the £250 floor.
4. Document the selected buffer in the monthly Affordability Check and Plan.

For November, those conditions pass. The selected £250 buffer is therefore appropriate for this Plan. The £1,000 elevated-risk rule remains unchanged.
