## Calibration Principles

1. The locked November Assumptions continue the £1,900 cash-rail pilot: Monzo Joint £950, Romeo Starling £475 and Kelly Starling £475.
2. Cash-rail depletion is the primary category-level reference because it reflects the funded pilot more directly than the mixed-period card statements.
3. Card observations supplement the depletion view but are not added to it; doing so could double count purchases or compare different periods.
4. The category mix follows the existing Monzo and Starling pot structure so every limit has a defined payment rail.
5. Credit-card availability does not add spending capacity. Any eligible card purchase consumes the matching funded bucket.
6. Interest and debt repayments are not controllable-spend buckets.
7. Mortgage, Santander recurring outflows, fixed current-account DDs, tithe, and card/BT obligations remain outside these limits.
8. Travel and pickleball sinking pots remain separate from monthly spendable buckets and are target-based rather than automatic monthly contributions.
9. Limits are calibrated before affordability is tested.
